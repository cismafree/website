Who we are

CismaFree is a small software studio run by a sole trader in England. For the information described here, CismaFree is the data controller under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), and is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us, plus the basic technical data any website receives.

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message, and if you add them, your business name and phone number. We also note which language you used the site in.
  • Emails you send us: your email address and whatever you include.
  • Technical data: when you visit, your device sends its IP address and browser details to the services that host the site and load its fonts. We don’t run analytics or advertising trackers.

We don’t sell your information, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Who we share it with

We use a few trusted services to run the site. They handle data on our behalf:

  • FormSubmit passes contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Google provides our email (Gmail) and the website fonts (Google Fonts).
  • GitHub hosts the website, and Porkbun manages our domain.

Some of these services store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by UK adequacy regulations (such as the UK–US data bridge) or by contract terms approved for international transfers. We would only share your information with anyone else if the law required it.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to work together: up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Customer records: for as long as you’re a customer, then 6 years for tax and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or give you a copy to take elsewhere. You can also object to us using it. Email us and we’ll reply within one month. It’s free.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and storage

cismafree.com doesn’t set tracking cookies. It saves one small setting in your browser to remember whether you chose English or Portuguese. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Our products

Each CismaFree product has its own website with its own terms and privacy notice, which explain how that product handles data. This notice covers cismafree.com only.
